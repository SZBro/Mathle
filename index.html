Mathle

How to play

  • Arrange all the tiles into one expression that meets today's goal.
  • Drag tiles into the box, or tap a tile to place it. Tap a placed tile to send it back.
  • No values are shown while you build. Work it out in your head or in the scratch box.
  • You get one submit. Then you'll see the steps, the best possible answer and your score.
  • Order: parentheses, then functions (√ ∛ log), then ^, then × ÷, then + −. A function applies only to the next number or ( … ) group: √9 ^ 2 = 9.
  • − always subtracts; it's never a negative sign.
